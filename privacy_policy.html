Privacy Policy for Studio Management

Effective Date: October 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

Summary: Studio Management operates on an offline-first architecture. Your business data (customers, events, payments, crew) is stored strictly locally on your device and is never uploaded, tracked, or sold to external servers by us.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Studio Management ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our"), designed and published by Maruti Photo Studio. We understand the importance of privacy and are deeply committed to protecting the confidential information of photography studios, photographers, and their clients.

This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles, processes, and protects your information when you download, install, and use Studio Management.

2. Data Collection and Usage

A. Information You Provide in the App:

  • Customer & Booking Details: Customer name, phone number, WhatsApp contact, event dates, event venues, photography packages, and event requirements.
  • Financial Records: Package rates, advance deposits received, remaining balances, payment status, and invoices.
  • Crew & Freelancer Details: Crew names, contact numbers, roles (Photographer, Drone Pilot, Cinematographer, Editor), and per-day charges.
  • Studio Profile: Studio branding name, owner name, contact numbers, and studio address.

Where is this stored? All of the above data is stored exclusively in a local database (SQLite via Android Room) on your physical Android device. We do not transmit or store this information on any external cloud server operated by us.

3. Advertisements & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

Studio Management uses Google AdMob to deliver in-app advertisements (Banner and Interstitial ads). In order to display relevant ads, Google AdMob and its partners may automatically collect certain device and network data as permitted by Android OS:

  • Advertising ID: Google Advertising ID (AAID) for ad personalization, frequency capping, and fraud prevention.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, carrier, and language settings.
  • Diagnostic & Performance Data: Ad load times, ad impressions, and crash logs.

Google's handling of collected advertising data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Technologies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google AdMob Privacy & Policy Details

4. Android Permissions Requested

Studio Management requests only minimal permissions necessary to function:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to connect to Google AdMob ad networks and display advertisements.
  • READ_CONTACTS: Used strictly to allow you to select and import client or crew contact details directly from your phone address book into the application. We do not sync, store, or upload your contacts to any server.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13 (API 33) and above to send notifications for upcoming photography event schedules and task reminders.
  • VIBRATE: Used for tactile feedback when saving events or receiving schedule alerts.
  • Photo Picker (Zero-Permission): Used for selecting studio logos or banner images via Android Photo Picker without requiring broad storage permissions.

5. Data Backup and Export

The application provides an offline JSON backup and restore feature. When you create a backup, the file is saved locally to your device's private storage or selected folder. You have total authority over how and where you store or share your backup files.

6. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or share your personal, business, customer, or financial records with any third party for marketing or commercial purposes. Data is only processed locally for your studio operations, and through Google AdMob solely for advertising operations as described above.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all application data resides on your physical Android device:

  • You can delete any customer, event booking, or crew member record at any time directly through the app UI.
  • You can permanently wipe all stored data by going to Android Settings > Apps > Studio Management > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the application from your device will delete all local databases and associated files.

8. Children's Privacy

Studio Management is a professional studio business management tool intended for photographers and studio owners aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

9. Security

We value your trust. Your business and client records remain confined within your device's sandbox environment enforced by Android security standards. We encourage users to protect their devices with screen locks, PINs, or biometrics to safeguard their local business information.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date" and included in updated versions of the application.
