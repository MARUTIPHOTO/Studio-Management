Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Studio Management

Effective Date: October 2026

Summary: Studio Management operates offline-first. Your customer and event data is stored exclusively on your physical device. We never sell or share your business data with third parties.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Studio Management ("the Application"), developed by Maruti Photo Studio. We value the privacy of your studio operations and client relationships.

2. Data Collection and Usage

  • Customer & Booking Details: Customer name, phone, WhatsApp contact, event date, venue, packages.
  • Financial Records: Package rates, advances, dues, and payment statuses.
  • Crew & Freelancer Details: Crew names, contact numbers, roles, and charges.
  • Studio Profile: Studio branding name, owner name, contact numbers, and studio address.

Local Storage: All business data is stored locally on your device in an encrypted Room SQLite database. We do not operate external cloud servers storing your client records.

3. Advertisements & Google AdMob

The app integrates Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial ads. Google AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage metrics strictly to serve ads in compliance with Google Play Developer policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Ad Technologies

4. Permissions

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: For loading AdMob advertising.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: For photography event schedule reminders.
  • VIBRATE: For alert feedback.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can delete individual customer or event records at any time from within the app, or delete all data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.
